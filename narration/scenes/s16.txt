[[PCBM-h adds an {k:extra path} for evidence the concepts miss]] The concept list may miss useful evidence in the image. The hybrid post-hoc concept bottleneck model, or PCBM-h, adds a residual: an extra linear head that reads the same image features directly. Add its class scores to the concept head's scores, then apply softmax. For our zebra, the score is about 2.03 plus 0.31, giving 2.34 before softmax. During training, keep the concept model fixed and fit only the extra head using class labels. At test time, both paths read the same image.
