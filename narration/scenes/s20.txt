## s20 | Leakage in a trained model | 0.4 | 0.8
[[In a trained model, the {c:score} for large rises with the radius]] Does a real trained model leak? Take a small model trained on synthetic shapes, which Part 11 looks at closely, and its concept large, labelled 1 for a radius of 14 pixels or more. Its score rises smoothly with the radius: about 0.09 at 12 pixels, 0.25 at 13, 0.63 at 14, and 0.87 at 15. So it carries a graded size, more than large or not large. || 1.0
[[Position and shade leave almost no trace: probe the {c:scores}]] A simple linear probe recovers essentially nothing about the shape's position or shade from the scores. That is how to look for leakage: probe what the scores predict beyond their labels. This is one small synthetic model. || 1.0
[[Hard {c:concepts} pass only 0 or 1, and lose accuracy]] Hard concepts pass only 0 or 1 to the head, so nothing extra travels. On our CUB run, a linear head on hard concepts gets about 53 percent of the test photos right; on soft scores, 69 percent. || 0.8
[[Fit the {f:head} on true {c:concepts}; remove or report {k:side paths}]] Independent training fits the head on true binary concepts, so it never learns hidden detail in the scores. Side paths can be removed, or reported clearly. || 0.8
[[Above all, measure]] Above all, measure: probe the scores, and test whether interventions help on held-out data. || 1.2
