## s49 | Rung two C · Learn from grounded concept targets | 0.4 | 0.8
[[C · Ground {c:concepts} in image regions]] Group C also learns a concept predictor, while remaining on rung two. It uses a different source of training targets: instead of copying image-text similarities, use a pretrained detector to locate concepts in image regions and provide concept targets. Train the concept layer from these targets, then train the class head from class labels. At test time, the learned concept predictor and head process the new image; the detector supplies training supervision. Vision-language-guided concept bottleneck models are our example. Grounding adds a spatial check, although detector errors can still be learned. || 0.8
