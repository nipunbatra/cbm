[[Rung two: where do the concept values come from?]] Both approaches on rung two still train a class head from class-labelled images. They avoid human concept labels on every task image. We will organise them by one design choice: where do the concept values come from? In group A, keep CLIP similarities as the concepts: our phrase-based PCBM example and Language in a Bottle. In group B, train concepts from targets grounded in image regions: vision-language-guided concept bottleneck models. These are alternatives within one rung, not successive training steps. || 0.8
