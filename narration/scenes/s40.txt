## s40 | Part 10 · Rung two: a map of the approaches | 0.4 | 0.8
[[Rung two: where do the concept values come from?]] All approaches on rung two still train a class head from class-labelled images. They avoid human concept labels on every task image. We will organise them by one design choice: where do the concept values come from? In group A, keep CLIP similarities as the concepts: our phrase-based PCBM example and Language in a Bottle. In group B, train a concept layer to imitate CLIP: label-free concept bottleneck models. In group C, train concepts from targets grounded in image regions: vision-language-guided concept bottleneck models. These are alternatives within one rung, not successive training steps. || 0.8
