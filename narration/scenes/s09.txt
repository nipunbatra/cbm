## s09 | Concept leakage | 0.4 | 0.8
[[A {c:concept score} can carry more than its name says]] One caution: a concept score can carry more than its name says. Imagine a score called stripes present that also carries extra information about the zebra species. If the task head uses that extra information, we call it concept leakage. The named concept then does not fully explain what the model uses. Different confidence values alone do not prove leakage. The practical takeaway is to test whether correcting a concept has the intended effect on the prediction. || 1.2
