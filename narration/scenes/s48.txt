## s48 | Rung two B · Learn a concept predictor from CLIP | 0.4 | 0.8
[[B · Let {x:CLIP} teach a {c:concept predictor}]] We stay on rung two, but change how a new image gets its concept values. Group A used CLIP similarities directly. Group B uses CLIP as a teacher during training: its scores train a concept layer on another image encoder. At test time, the image encoder and learned concept layer produce the concept values, and the class head predicts the class. The CLIP teacher is no longer needed for prediction. Class labels still train the head. Label-free concept bottleneck models are our example of this design. || 0.8
