How can a person inspect, and correct, the evidence a neural network uses? Concept bottleneck models predict through a layer of named, human-readable concepts that can be read, evaluated and edited. A graduate lecture film for ES 667 Deep Learning at IIT Gandhinagar.

Covers: a real CLIP black box and its occlusion map; the concept bottleneck x → c → y; worked concept-to-class arithmetic; independent, sequential and joint training; human interventions; why a correct edit can hurt; incomplete vocabularies; concept leakage with a measured probe; worked examples of concept embedding models, post-hoc CBMs, label-free CBMs and LaBo (real CLIP and language-model outputs on 36 photographs); evaluation; and scientific sensing.
